<p>GymTracker is a workout tracker that keeps your data on your phone.</p>
<p><b>Track workouts</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Start from a routine or an empty workout, and add exercises on the fly</li>
<li>Each exercise is done by reps or for a time, with or without weight</li>
<li>Edit weights, reps and times freely as you go; tick a set when it's done</li>
<li>Rest timer starts on its own, with a countdown tick and a boxing bell at the end; timed sets have their own countdown</li>
<li>See what you did last time; weights are pre-filled from it</li>
<li>Type weights in kg or lb: they're converted for you</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Routines and exercises</b>: comes with about 60 common exercises; add your own with the muscles they work.</p>
<p><b>Progress</b>: a chart per exercise (estimated one-rep max, heaviest weight, volume, reps or time), personal records, workouts per week or month, and sets per muscle each week. Drag the charts to look further back.</p>
<p><b>Your data</b>: JSON backup and restore to move to a new phone, export to Excel (.xlsx) for your own analysis. No account, no ads, no tracking.</p>
